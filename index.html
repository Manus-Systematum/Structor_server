A companion for Warhammer 40,000, 11th edition

Structor builds army lists under the 11th edition detachment rules, and runs beside a game: mission setup, the stratagems available in each phase, victory points, and the statlines you need mid-turn. It works offline and keeps everything on the device.

In testing on iOS. Not released yet — there is no download on this page.

Building a list

  • Thirty-five armies and the core rules are bundled with the app. No account, no download step.
  • Detachment points, enhancement slots, unit caps and wargear limits are checked while the list is built, at the battle size chosen.
  • A price that cannot be resolved is reported as a problem rather than counted as zero, so an incomplete list never reads as a cheap one.
  • Lists exported as plain text from another builder can be pasted in. Names that do not match are listed rather than dropped silently.
  • A saved list keeps a copy of the data it was built from, so a points update changes what you see next time you edit it, not what you already wrote down.

During a game

  • Mission, deployment zones and terrain layout, drawn to scale from the published layout data.
  • Each phase lists the stratagems usable in it, with the full printed text and the cost. Using one deducts the CP and records which unit used it, so the same unit cannot quietly use two.
  • Primary and secondary scoring in the card's own wording, with the running total.
  • Unit cards with per-model statlines, wounds and models remaining, and abilities attributed to the datasheet that grants them — a leader's invulnerable save is not silently shared with the squad it joined.

What it does not do

  • Ability and mission descriptions are summaries written by the data projects, not Games Workshop's printed wording. They are enough to play from; for a rules dispute, use the card or the book.
  • Some of the data is still on a pre-launch provisional dataslate carried over from 10th edition. The app marks that where it applies rather than presenting it as current.
  • Lists cannot yet be sent between devices.
  • Nothing is collected. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and no part of the app needs a network connection.

Where the data comes from

Structor holds no rules data of its own. Everything it shows comes from the community projects below, and their terms are the terms this app ships under.

  • Powered by 40kdc-data

    40kdc.alpacasoft.dev

    Datasheets, weapons, abilities, missions, and stratagem costs and phases. Licensed CC BY 4.0 © Alpaca Software and the 40kdc community contributors; its schemas are CC0. Changes were made: the data is repackaged into compressed bundles, corrected in a recorded set of places where it is wrong, and rendered by the app.

  • Powered by Wahapedia

    wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed

    The rules text of the stratagems, from Wahapedia's published data export, which asks to be credited in this phrasing.

  • Stratagem card generator

    github.com/pguetschow/warhammer-40k-stratagem-card-generator

    The eleven Core Stratagems, transcribed there from the free Warhammer 40,000 Core Rules. MIT licensed.

  • BSData / wh40k-11e

    github.com/BSData/wh40k-11e

    Datasheet and ability text. The repository carries no licence file. Asked directly, the maintainers replied that the organisation often does not attach one, that the repository is intended to be open source, and that it may be cloned and altered freely (issue 918). That is permission rather than a licence, and it is recorded here as the weaker thing it is.

  • BSData / wh40k-11e-mfm

    github.com/BSData/wh40k-11e-mfm

    Games Workshop's published Munitorum points, parsed from mfm.warhammer-community.com and used as an independent check on the points above. MIT licensed.

  • Warhammer Community downloads

    warhammer-community.com

    Games Workshop's own free Faction Packs. The community sources above publish 11th-edition records ahead of the printed rules, and when a rules update lands they are wrong in two directions at once: missing the wording, and listing stratagems the released detachment does not have. Those corrections are read from the packs, shipped as a separate data update, and removed once the sources above publish the same rules. Games Workshop grant no licence for these; they are used as published.

  • gdmissions.app

    gdmissions.app

    The sentence printed on each mission card, where the app would otherwise show a paraphrase. The site publishes no licence, terms or attribution notice of any kind, so this credit is the only footing the use has.

Everything the app knows about the game was collected from the openly published sources named above, some of them Games Workshop's own free downloads.
